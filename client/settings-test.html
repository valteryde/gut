    What gut can do
    
      
    
      
    
        
    

          
    
            
    Gut is an agent that works a real computer
              for you — describe an outcome in chat and it drives a desktop of
              Chrome and LibreOffice to get there, checking in when it has
              something to say.
    
          
    

          
    
            
    The desktop
    
            
      
              
    • A real machine. Every device runs a Linux
                desktop with Chrome and LibreOffice — the agent sees the screen
                and works the mouse and keyboard. Watch it live on the right.
      • 
              
    • Files in, deliverables out. Attach with the
                paperclip, drop files on the chat, or paste an image — it reads
                them and sends back documents, spreadsheets and screenshots.
      • 
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    You stay in control
    
            
      
              
    • Take over anytime. Click into the screen to
                handle a login, 2FA or CAPTCHA yourself — Resume hands control
                back.
      • 
              
    • Queue & steer mid-run. Keep typing while
                it works — messages queue for the next task, or ⚡ /
                ⌘/Ctrl+Enter injects at the agent's next step.
      • 
              
    • Quiet until it matters. It checks in at
                milestones, asks when it's stuck, and sends a notification when
                a task finishes.
      • 
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Bigger jobs
    
            
      
              
    • Plans, not surprises. Long tasks get a plan
                card and a live checklist pinned above the chat — and near the
                context limit, history compacts instead of killing the run.
      • 
              
    • Helpers in parallel. Background subagents can
                do web research and shell work — optionally on a cheaper model —
                while the agent keeps driving.
      • 
              
    • Pick up where you left off. Conversations
                persist per device with their full context — reopen one from
                ☰ and keep talking.
      • 
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Your setup
    
            
      
              
    • Bring your own model. Claude, GPT, Gemini,
                DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or local weights via Ollama — picked per
                conversation, keys under Provider keys.
      • 
              
    • Many desktops at once. Add backends as
                devices — local or a VPS — and each runs its own agent in
                parallel; hop between them from the chip in the composer.
      • 
              
    • See every cent. The pill in the top bar tracks
                live dollars and tokens per session and all-time — hover it for
                the breakdown.
